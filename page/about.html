Version 0.2 Beta - What's New

Acki Version 0.2 Beta focuses on improving the overall experience, making the website easier to use and giving users a more personal community space.

  • Personal Profile - Users can have their own profile page and share their identity with the community.
  • Profile Picture - Users can upload and change their own profile picture.
  • Post Management - Users can edit and delete their own posts.
  • Improved Comments - Conversations are easier and more organized.
  • Better User Experience - Improved interface and overall usability.

What is this website?

Acki is a calm community where people can share ideas, artwork, stories, questions, and everyday moments without pressure. We believe every post has value, whether it's a masterpiece or just a random thought.
